About the service

Simple pizza delivery for busy days

PizzaGo is designed for students and young professionals who want a quick, clear and reliable way to order food without calling a restaurant.

  • Clear menu with prices and ingredients.
  • Simple cart and one-page checkout.
  • Order tracking with an easy progress timeline.
  • Responsive layout for desktop, tablet and mobile.
